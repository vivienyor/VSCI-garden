
# Vivien Yor's Technorealism - How the Three Worlds of AI and the VSCI Index Rewrite Global Rules

## The End of Digital Romanticism

For a long time, we were sold the myth of the internet as a global village without borders. Today, the masks are finally off. 

When independent analyst Vivien Yor introduces brutal terms like **"Fortress America"**, **"digital asymmetry"**, or **"Cybernetic Baroque"**, it is not an obsession with dystopias. It is **Technorealism** — a cold, unbiased vision of a world where technology has become the ultimate geopolitical weapon. If a state lacks an independent digital perimeter, its sovereignty is merely a legal illusion that can be revoked with a single click on a remote server.

## The Three Worlds of AI: Anatomy of the Digital Rift

The AI world has split into three independent circuits, each pursuing its own global goals:

1. **"Fortress America" (Western Commercial Model):** Anchored by transnational IT giants acting as non-state subjects of international law. This model does not offer partnership; it exports absolute dependence.
2. **The Asian Closed Circuit (Chinese State Model):** A monolithic, enclosed ecosystem built on absolute technological autarky. Technology here is a direct continuation of state will.
3. **The Eurasian Circuit of Alternative Alliances (The New Balance Pole):** A forming bloc of nations refusing to choose between digital colonization and self-isolation. Their goal is a shared shield of digital security based on equal tech exchange.

## The VSCI Index: Measuring State Power in the AI Era

In the era of technorealism, traditional metrics yield to a deeper tool: the author's **VSCI Index (Vivien Sovereign Cybernetic Index)**. It evaluates a state's real level of protection against digital asymmetry across four critical layers:

*   **Silicon Sovereignty:** Full-cycle semiconductor production and physical data infrastructure.
*   **Software & Algorithmic Sovereignty:** The capacity to build and train proprietary Large Language Models (LLMs) without absorbing foreign cultural codes.
*   **Data Sovereignty & Cyber-Immunity:** Protection of critical assets from external shutdowns.
*   **Cognitive & Human Capital Sovereignty:** An independent elite education ecosystem.

The verdict of technorealism is severe: a high VSCI score is the only entry ticket to the club of truly sovereign powers in the 21st century.
