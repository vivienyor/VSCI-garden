# Core Concepts & VSCI Matrix

The operational core of our analysis relies on the **Vivien Sovereign Cybernetic Index (VSCI)**. It mathematically models the real level of technological insulation and infrastructure autarky of a given sovereign entity.

---

### 📊 The VSCI Matrix Balance

The absolute index score is synthesized as a geometric mean across the core structural volumes of sovereignty, adjusting for critical infrastructural dependencies:

$$\text{VSCI} = \sqrt[4]{\text{Silicon} \times \text{Software} \times \text{Energy} \times \text{Data}}$$

Where each vector represents a normalized coordinate from $0.0$ to $1.0$ within the absolute perimeter of technorealism.
