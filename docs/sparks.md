# ⚡ Cybernetic Sparks

Tactical updates, raw monitoring flashes, and immediate observations from the theater of global technorealism.

---

### [Spark #04] • The Cloud Illusion
The myth of borderless cloud neutrality collapses the exact second a kinetic or economic conflict begins. Virtual servers do not exist. There is only hardware owned by someone else, sitting in a building protected by a specific military perimeter.

### [Spark #03] • Algorithmic Feudalism
Modern Big Tech platforms do not behave like democratic institutions or classic corporations. They operate as 17th-century European courts. Ornate, hyper-complex user interfaces and endless user agreements are merely the digital equivalent of baroque court protocols designed to mask absolute, unchecked power.

### [Spark #02] • The Telemetry Leash
Any infrastructure that requires an active external connection to verify its license or push an update is not sovereign. It is a hostage on a digital leash. True technological insulation begins with the complete eradication of outbound automated telemetry.

### [Spark #01] • Cognitive Autarky
Sovereign silicon and software are completely useless if the engineers operating them are trained within a foreign cognitive ecosystem. Human capital remains the ultimate, most vulnerable layer of the VSCI matrix.
