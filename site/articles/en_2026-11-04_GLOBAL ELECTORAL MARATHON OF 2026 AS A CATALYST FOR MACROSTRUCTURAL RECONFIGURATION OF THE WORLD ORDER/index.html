    ABSTRACT

    This monograph provides a comprehensive, neo-institutional, and geoeconomic analysis of the global electoral cycle of 2026, examining its impact on planetary security, resource allocation, and technological sovereignty. The study decodes the systemic implications of the U.S. midterm elections (November 3, 2026), which resulted in a decisive victory for the Democratic Party, establishing total legislative control over both chambers of the U.S. Congress and reducing the Donald Trump administration to an institutional "lame duck" operating under Scenario B ("Mr. Veto").

    The research synchronizes these domestic American shifts with structural ruptures across Europe—specifically the collapse of the Franco-German-Spanish FCAS sixth-generation fighter project and Germany's pivot toward the UK-led GCAP alliance, which has effectively split the continent into two divergent technopolitical axes: the autarkic "Latina Axis" (Paris) and the integrated "Northern Axis" (London/Berlin). Concurrently, the paper evaluates the limits of conventional deterrence in the Middle East following the unpublicized U.S. CENTCOM kinetic strike on Iran’s Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, analyzing Iran’s asymmetric response matrix and its newly consolidated threshold nuclear status under the complete dismantling of UN Resolution 2231.

    Finally, the study deconstructs the strategic adaptation of the Russian Federation and the People's Republic of China, demonstrating how Moscow and Beijing are migrating the "Energy Triangle" (Russia-USA-Europe) into the "deep shadow" of OFAC temporary waivers while exploiting the political paralysis in Washington to build non-dollar clearing frameworks and advance "grey-zone" operations, as evidenced by the PRC's maritime chokehold near the Pratas (Dongsha) Islands and the systemic supply-chain infiltration of Taiwanese defense consortia (Align Corp). Utilizing the analytical framework of transactional realism, the author projects three macro-political scenarios for the evolution of the global architecture up to 2035, concluding that real power in the current era belongs not to resource owners or end consumers, but to the operators of transactional pipelines, tech stacks, and algorithmic standards.

    INTRODUCTION

    By late November 2026, the global political system concluded an unprecedentedly intense and extensive electoral cycle. A series of planetary-scale elections spanning the Euro-Atlantic architecture, the Balkan region, Eastern Europe, and the Asia-Pacific region has definitively dismantled the remains of post-Cold War and neoliberal normativity. The electoral processes of 2026 did not merely serve as regular mechanisms for the elite rotation; rather, they acted as powerful triggers and markers of deep, latent, tectonic shifts in the very nature of state sovereignty.

    The central milestones of this marathon, which predetermined the contours of the planetary balance of power, include:

    1. The U.S. Midterm Elections (November 3, 2026): Concluding with a crushing defeat for the Republican Party and the establishment of total Democratic control over Capitol Hill, reducing Donald Trump's executive branch to a "lame duck."
    2. The Presidential Election in Bulgaria: Marked by the victory of Iliana Iotova (57.57% of the vote), who became the first female president in the country's history and solidified a regional demand for pragmatic stabilization in the Balkan node.
    3. Electoral Ruptures in Europe: Large-scale shifts in Hungary (Péter Magyar and the Tisza party challenging Viktor Orbán's consensus), Serbia (the disintegration of Aleksandar Vučić's 14-year era under the pressure of mass movements), and municipal and parliamentary fluctuations in France and Portugal, leading to an institutional split in the European defense sector over sixth-generation fighter projects (GCAP vs. FCAS).
    4. Synchronous Escalation in Critical Chokepoints: The unpublicized U.S. CENTCOM defensive strike on Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz and the PRC's accelerated "grey-zone" operations in the Taiwan Strait (the incident near the Pratas Islands) occurring precisely during Washington's election-induced institutional vacuum.

    This monograph provides a multi-layered political and neo-institutional analysis of the outcomes of the global electoral marathon of 2026. The focus is directed toward decoding the institutional traps of divided government, auditing the transformation of macro-regional technological sovereignty, and designing long-term predictive scenarios for the world order up to 2035 through the lens of transactional realism.

    To interpret the combined results of the global electoral marathon, classical behavioral and realist paradigms prove cognitively limited. This study deploys a multidimensional methodological synthesis combining historical neo-institutionalism (D. North, B. Weingast), the concept of path dependence, veto-players theory (G. Tsebelis), and the paradigm of transactional realism.

The electoral upheavals of 2026 verified this thesis: the democratic victory in the U.S. Congress or Iotova's presidency in Bulgaria are battles not over physical supply routes, but over the right to authorize and collect rent from transactional channels.

    1. THE AMERICAN INDENTATION: MIDTERMS-2026 AND THE PARALYSIS OF "DIVIDED GOVERNMENT"

    The primary planetary event that predetermined the temporal and qualitative parameters of the global transit was the U.S. midterm elections on November 3, 2026. The American electoral system demonstrated the classic compensatory swing against a monopoly of party control.

    Parametric Outlines of the Republican Defeat:

    • The U.S. Congress: The Democratic Party established complete legislative control over Capitol Hill. In the Senate, the Democratic caucus secured an absolute majority of 52 seats (a net gain of +6), shrinking the Republican presence to 41 seats. In the House of Representatives, the Democrats comfortably surpassed the 218-seat threshold, flipping more than 22 districts.
    • Collapse of Traditional Geography: Historic Democratic flips of Senate seats in Texas and Ohio, alongside a sensational pickup in right-conservative Kansas for the first time in nearly a 100-year interval, demonstrated the demographic and economic erosion of the Republican base. Furthermore, the total sweep by Democrats of all statewide offices in Georgia marked a permanent structural shift in the American South.

    Personal Audit of the New Speaker Vertical:

    Power on Capitol Hill materializes through the leadership of key committees, which has transitioned to fierce opponents of the Donald Trump administration:

    1. Hakeem Jeffries (Speaker of the House): Consolidates control over the legislative agenda, effectively ruling out the passage of right-conservative budgets.
    2. Jack Reed (Senate Armed Services Committee): Establishes absolute parliamentary oversight over CENTCOM and Pentagon operations, blocking uncoordinated kinetic actions.
    3. Ben Cardin / Chris Coons (Senate Foreign Relations Committee): Block any attempts by the White House to unilaterally rewrite international treaties or lift sanctions regimes without Senate approval.

    The institutional consequence is clear: Donald Trump has been reduced to a "lame duck." The implementation of Scenario B ("Mr. Veto") means that for the next two years, U.S. foreign policy becomes a hostage to Washington's domestic trench warfare, and the threat of the House launching a third impeachment inquiry remains a permanent tool of institutional blackmail against the executive branch.

    1. THE EUROPEAN SCHISM: THE COLLAPSE OF STRATEGIC AUTONOMY

    Simultaneously with the American indentation, the European continent passed through a point of critical divergence, registering the final collapse of a unified European defense-industrial and technological space. The structural rupture occurred with the official collapse of the flagship Franco-German-Spanish sixth-generation fighter project—FCAS (Future Combat Air System)—in June 2026, caused by irreconcilable contradictions between Dassault Aviation and Airbus Defense and Space regarding intellectual property rights (IP-rights).

    The maneuver executed by German Chancellor Friedrich Merz and British Prime Minister Andy Burnham in late October 2026 to launch formal talks on Germany's accession to the alternative GCAP (Global Combat Air Programme) consortium alongside the UK, Italy, and Japan revealed a profound mental and technological chasm. The continent has split into two macro-regional axes with fundamentally antithetical philosophies of sovereignty.

    Legal Vacuum and Iran's Threshold Nuclear Status:

    The ultimate institutional challenge to long-term stability is the transition of Iran's nuclear program into an unverified phase following the formal expiration of UN Security Council Resolution 2231 (October 18, 2025). In an environment of absolute legal vacuum and the "blinding" of IAEA monitoring systems following Israeli bombardments, Iran has de facto solidified its status as a "threshold nuclear state." Its stockpiles of 60%-enriched uranium are mathematically sufficient to fabricate several nuclear explosive devices within a breakout time measured in days.

    The Republican defeat opens a unique window of opportunity for Tehran in the second half of November 2026: knowing that the new Democratic majority will freeze additional appropriations for large-scale conventional warfare and launch investigations into the unauthorized Qeshm strike, Iran can activate its proxy pressure through the Iraqi theater and Bahrain by fiat, without fearing a pre-emptive U.S. strike on its hardened nuclear sites (Mount Pickaxe).

    1. THE GEOECONOMIC MANEUVER: RUSSIAN AND CHINESE STRATEGIC ADAPTATION

    For the Russian Federation and the People's Republic of China, the outcomes of the 2026 electoral marathon served as a signal to accelerate the dismantling of dollar-centric, transparent regulatory mechanisms in international trade.

    The Russian Federation: Migrating the "Energy Triangle" Into the Shadows

    The official stance of the Kremlin, articulated by Dmitry Peskov ("This is an internal U.S. matter, we are watching closely"), camouflages a calculated reconfiguration of export pipelines coordinated by Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak.

    • Recognizing that the Democratic Congress will block permanent sanctions waivers, Russia is shifting the execution of the "Energy Triangle" (Russia-USA-Europe) into the sector of temporary OFAC licenses and private consulting arrangements.
    • Because the physical volume of the resource (80–100 billion cubic meters of gas per year, equivalent to the peak of Nord Stream 2) remains indispensable for the survival of the European industrial core, Moscow accepts the rules of "legal camouflage." Russian operators embed a risk premium but preserve stable export revenues, shifting the schedules for full capacity utilization toward early 2028.

    The People's Republic of China: "Grey-Zone" Dominance and Financial Autonomy

    Beijing, under the leadership of Xi Jinping and Foreign Minister Wang Yi, is utilizing the paralysis of the "lame duck" in the White House to advance its interests ahead of the APEC and G20 summits.

    • The Taiwan Strait Rupture: Synchronously with the U.S. elections, the PRC Coast Guard and PLA assets accelerated pressure in the Taiwan Strait. A sharp incident near the Pratas (Dongsha) Islands, where Chinese enforcement vessels closed within less than 1 nautical mile of Taiwanese interceptors, coincided with a structural crisis within Taipei's own defense-industrial base. Prosecutorial raids and arrests of executives at Align Corp and Jiin Ming Industry exposed the systemic use of PRCchips in Taiwanese military UAVs, completely collapsing the state's flagship red-free supply chain program.
    • Beijing is effectively presenting Trump with a transactional trade-off: maritime logistical stability in exchange for lifting U.S. semiconductor export controls. Simultaneously, Vice Premier He Lifeng is forcing the creation of independent BRICS+ clearinghouses and insurance pools, migrating Chinese crude purchases (4.5 million barrels per day through Hormuz) into national currencies.

    CONCLUSION

    The institutional outcomes of the global electoral marathon of 2026 demonstrate the clear limits of individual transactional pragmatism when operating within the rigid institutional design of Anglo-Saxon constitutional models. Voters stripped Donald Trump of his legislative monopoly, transforming the final two years of his presidential term into an exhausting war of position with Congress.

    The paradigm of technological and resource sovereignty allows for a definitive conclusion: the democratic victory in Midterms-2026 marked the global system's entry into a state of structural transaction friction and permanent legal uncertainty. The signature of the U.S. president under international pacts and Energy Triangle deals is no longer a sovereign guarantee of execution; it has been reduced to an opening bid for an exhausting legislative auction on Capitol Hill. In this environment, the Russian Federation and the People's Republic of China are demonstrating high adaptability, moving their geoeconomic maneuvers into "grey zones" of law and constructing independent financial infrastructure for the Global South.

    The planetary architecture up to 2035 will evolve within a paradigm of rigid coalition balancing, where the capacity of macro-regional blocks to maintain the technological sovereignty of their core, while preserving structural agility within networks of distributed interdependence, becomes the sole and non-negotiable condition for preserving genuine state subjectivity in the XXI century.
